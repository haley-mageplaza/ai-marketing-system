# SP Hide Price & Access Control

## App Identity
- Name: SP Hide Price & Access Control
- Product line: B2B
- Shopify App Store URL: https://apps.shopify.com/hide-price-access-control

## Purpose
Control pricing visibility — hide prices from guests or specific customer segments to protect margins and manage B2B pricing strategies.

## Key Use Cases
- Hide prices from non-logged-in visitors
- Show different pricing to different customer groups
- Control access to specific products or collections
- Protect wholesale pricing from public visibility

## Positioning
- Problem: B2B merchants need to control who sees their pricing
- Outcome: Protect margins and manage pricing visibility without complex workarounds
