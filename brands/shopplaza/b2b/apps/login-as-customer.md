# SP Login As Customer

## App Identity
- Name: SP Login As Customer
- Product line: B2B
- Shopify App Store URL: https://apps.shopify.com/sp-login-as-customer

## Purpose
Allow store admins and support staff to log in as a customer to troubleshoot issues, place orders on their behalf, or verify the customer experience.

## Key Use Cases
- Support staff accessing customer accounts for troubleshooting
- Placing orders on behalf of wholesale customers
- Verifying customer-specific pricing and access rules

## Positioning
- Problem: B2B support teams need to see exactly what their customers see
- Outcome: Faster support resolution and better customer experience
