# Shopplaza B2B Audience

## Primary Segments

### Wholesale Brands
- Sell in bulk to retailers or resellers
- Need custom pricing per customer or group
- Require quote workflows for large orders

### Manufacturers
- Sell direct to retailers
- Need company account management
- Require access control and pricing tiers

### B2B Merchants on Shopify
- Transitioning from manual B2B processes
- Looking for Shopify-native B2B solutions
- Need to manage both B2B and B2C from one store

## B2B Pain Points
- Managing different pricing for different customers
- Handling quote requests manually
- Controlling who can see prices and products
- Onboarding wholesale customers with company accounts
- Shipping complexity for bulk/wholesale orders

## B2B Decision Factors
- Ease of setup and use
- Integration with existing Shopify store
- Ability to manage B2B and B2C in one store
- Support quality and availability
- App reliability and performance
