# Shopplaza B2B Context

## Overview

Shopplaza B2B is the primary marketing focus. These apps serve wholesale brands and B2B merchants on Shopify.

## B2B App Portfolio

See [product-portfolio.md](../brand/product-portfolio.md) for the complete B2B app catalog with URLs.

B2B apps focus on: pricing visibility control, quote workflows, customer account management, shipping rules, and wholesale pricing.

## Target Audience

- Wholesale brands and distributors
- Manufacturers selling direct to retailers
- B2B merchants needing pricing control, quote workflows, and customer segmentation

## B2B Messaging Focus

- Workflow automation
- Pricing control and visibility
- Operations efficiency
- B2B deal management

- Managing wholesale customers on Shopify
