# Shopplaza B2B Content Strategy

## B2B Content Focus

### Topics
- Workflow automation for wholesale
- Pricing control and visibility
- Access control and customer segmentation
- Quote management and negotiation
- Company account onboarding
- B2B operations on Shopify

### Content Types
- How-to guides (e.g., "How to set up B2B pricing on Shopify")
- Comparison articles (e.g., "Best Shopify apps for wholesale")
- Use case content (e.g., real merchant scenarios)
- Problem-solution articles (e.g., "How to manage wholesale customers on Shopify")

## B2B Content Rules

### Focus Areas
- Workflow
- Pricing

- Operations

### App Examples for B2B Content
- Request a quote

### Tone Adjustment for B2B
- More operational and process-oriented than B2C
- Emphasize efficiency and control
- Speak to business decision-makers, not just store admins
