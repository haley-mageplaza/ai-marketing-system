# Shopplaza B2B Integration Map

## Cross-App Integration

Shopplaza B2B apps are designed to work together. Key integration points:

### Pricing + Access Control
- Hide Price & Access Control + Wholesale Pricing
- Controls who sees prices and what pricing tier they receive

### Quote Workflow
- Request a Quote + Hide Price
- Hide prices from certain customers, then allow them to request custom quotes

### Customer Management
- Company Accounts + Login As Customer
- Manage B2B company profiles, allow support staff to log in as customers

### Order Fulfillment
- Smart Shipping + other order management workflows
- Batch orders and configure shipping rules for wholesale
