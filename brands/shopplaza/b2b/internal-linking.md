# Shopplaza B2B Internal Linking Strategy

## B2B Linking Topology

### Funnel Flow
- TOFU → MOFU: Link from educational B2B guides to "best apps" and comparison content
- MOFU → BOFU: Link from comparison content to B2B app pages and landing pages
- BOFU → Support: Link from app pages to related blogs and FAQs

### App Linking Rules
- Explain the B2B problem BEFORE linking to an app
- Link to the most relevant B2B app for the use case
- When discussing a B2B workflow, link to multiple related apps (e.g., Hide Price + Request a Quote)

### Cross-App Linking
B2B apps work together. Link between related apps when discussing:
- Pricing workflows (Hide Price ↔ Wholesale Pricing)
- Quote-to-order workflows (Request a Quote ↔ Company Accounts)
- Customer management (Login As Customer ↔ Company Accounts)
- Shipping (Smart Shipping ↔ other order management content)

### B2B Content Hub Structure
- Central B2B hub page links to all B2B content
- Each B2B app page links to related use case content
- Blog posts link to relevant app pages contextually

### Priority Links (B2B)
1. B2B app pages
2. B2B landing pages
3. B2B blog content
4. Cross-product-line content (only when directly relevant)
