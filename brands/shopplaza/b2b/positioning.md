# Shopplaza B2B Positioning

## B2B ASO Messaging

Focus areas:
- Workflow
- Pricing control
- Operations

Key messages:
- Manage wholesale customers
- Control pricing visibility
- Automate quote process

## Positioning Principles

- Lead with operational outcomes, not feature lists
- Emphasize B2B-specific pain points (pricing complexity, quote management, customer segmentation)
- Position apps as a connected B2B toolkit, not individual point solutions
- Differentiate from generic Shopify apps by showing B2B depth

## Feature-to-Benefit Translation (B2B)

Weak: "Hide price feature"
Strong: "Hide prices from guests or specific customers so you can control who sees your pricing and protect your margins"

Weak: "Request a quote app for Shopify"
Strong: "Let customers request quotes and negotiate pricing so you can close high-value B2B deals faster"
