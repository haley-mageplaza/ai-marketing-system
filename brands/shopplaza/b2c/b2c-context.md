# Shopplaza B2C Context

## Overview

Shopplaza B2C apps serve retail Shopify merchants focused on growing sales and conversions. These apps are managed by a separate team but are part of the Shopplaza portfolio.

## B2C App Portfolio

See [product-portfolio.md](../brand/product-portfolio.md) for the complete B2C app catalog with URLs.

B2C apps focus on: sizing accuracy, social proof, product feeds, digital products, and product discovery.

## Target Audience

- Retail merchants wanting to grow sales and conversions
- Stores focused on UX, engagement, and AOV optimization

## B2C Messaging Focus

- Conversion optimization
- UX improvement
- Sales growth
- Engagement and AOV

## B2C Content Themes

- Increasing AOV on Shopify
- Boosting customer engagement
- Improving shopping experience
- Social proof strategies
