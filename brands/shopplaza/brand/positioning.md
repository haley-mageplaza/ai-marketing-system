# Shopplaza Positioning

## Core Value Propositions

### A hub for all of your Shopify needs
One place for B2B & B2C apps, custom development, integrations, and support — so you don't juggle multiple vendors. Faster fixes, one bill, one roadmap.

### 5-star, quality apps that work together
Our apps are built to work seamlessly with each other, saving you setup headaches and ensuring a smooth experience for your customers. No more fighting with apps that don't play nice or syncing errors between tools.

### 12+ years of experience
With over a decade in eCommerce and Shopify, we understand what works and what doesn't. Our team has helped hundreds of merchants grow faster, avoid common pitfalls, and make Shopify work for both B2C and B2B.

### 24/7, dedicated support
Problems don't wait for business hours, and neither do we. Our support team is always ready to help — quickly, efficiently, and personally — so your store keeps running smoothly no matter the time.

## Messaging Principles

- Lead with outcomes ("Increase AOV") not features
- Benefit-driven headlines
- Include App Store ratings/reviews where relevant
- Primary CTA: "Try free on Shopify App Store"
