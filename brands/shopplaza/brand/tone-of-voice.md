# Shopplaza Tone of Voice

## Core Tone
- Friendly and modern
- Growth-focused
- Benefit-driven, energetic
- Practical, not theoretical

## Preferred Language
- "Grow your store"
- "Increase conversions"
- "Scale your store"
- "Simplify your workflow"
- "Turn visitors into buyers"

## Content Approach
- Lead with outcomes, not features
- Focus on merchant results
- Direct and actionable
- Problem → Solution → Outcome structure

## CTA Conventions
- Primary: "Try free on Shopify App Store"
- Placement: after solution sections, at the end, optional mid-content for long articles
