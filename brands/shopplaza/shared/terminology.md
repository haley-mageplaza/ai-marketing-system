# Shopplaza Terminology

## Brand Names
- **Shopplaza** — the brand name (not "ShopPlaza" or "Shop Plaza")
- **SP** prefix — used for B2B apps (e.g., SP Hide Price, SP Request a Quote)
- **MP** prefix — used for B2C apps (e.g., MP Size Chart, MP Instagram Feed)

## Product Line Terms
- **B2B** — wholesale, pricing control, B2B operations apps
- **B2C** — retail conversion, UX, engagement apps
- **Checkout** — pre-launch product line

## Common Terms
- **Merchant** — preferred over "user" or "customer" when referring to Shopify store owners
- **Install** — preferred over "download" for Shopify apps
- **App Store** — refers to Shopify App Store unless otherwise specified
- **Listing** — the app's page on the Shopify App Store

## CTA Conventions
- Primary: "Try free on Shopify App Store"
- App install: "Install" or "Try free"
- Content: "Learn more" or "See how it works"
