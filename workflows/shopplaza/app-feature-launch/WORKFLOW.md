# Workflow: Shopplaza App Feature Launch

## Purpose

Coordinate marketing for launching a new feature within an existing Shopplaza app.

## Required Context

- `brands/shopplaza/brand/` — brand identity and tone
- `brands/shopplaza/shared/` — content and ASO rules
- `brands/shopplaza/b2b/` or `brands/shopplaza/b2c/` — relevant product line
- `brands/shopplaza/b2b/apps/<app>.md` or `brands/shopplaza/b2c/apps/<app>.md` — specific app

## Relevant Skills

1. `/market launch <feature>` — generate a launch playbook
2. `/market aso <app-listing-url>` — update listing for the new feature
3. `/market copy <landing-url>` — optimize copy for the feature announcement
4. `/market emails <feature>` — announcement and adoption email sequences
5. `/market social <feature>` — social content for feature awareness

## Execution Sequence

1. **Launch Playbook** — Generate a timeline and checklist for the feature launch
2. **Listing Update Plan** — Recommend how to update the app listing to highlight the new feature
3. **Content Creation** — Blog post, social posts, and email announcing the feature
4. **Adoption Strategy** — Email sequences targeting existing users to drive feature adoption

## Expected Output

- `LAUNCH-PLAYBOOK.md` — week-by-week launch plan
- `ASO-AUDIT.md` — listing update recommendations
- `SOCIAL-CALENDAR.md` — launch social content
- `EMAIL-SEQUENCES.md` — announcement and adoption sequences

Save outputs to `outputs/shopplaza/`.
