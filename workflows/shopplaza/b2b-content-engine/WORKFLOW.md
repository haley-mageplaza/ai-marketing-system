# Workflow: Shopplaza B2B Content Engine

## Purpose

Ongoing content marketing workflow for the Shopplaza B2B product line. Drives organic traffic, builds topical authority, and supports app installs through the SEO-to-ASO bridge.

## Required Context

- `brands/shopplaza/brand/` — brand identity and tone
- `brands/shopplaza/shared/seo-rules.md` — SEO rules
- `brands/shopplaza/shared/content-rules.md` — content rules
- `brands/shopplaza/b2b/` — B2B context, funnel, content strategy, internal linking
- `brands/shopplaza/b2b/apps/` — relevant app context for content integration

## Relevant Skills

1. `/market seo <url>` — SEO audit of existing content
2. `/market copy <url>` — copy analysis and optimization
3. `/market social <topic>` — social content to promote blog posts
4. `/market competitors <url>` — content gap analysis against competitors
5. `/market brand <url>` — brand voice consistency check

## Execution Sequence

1. **Content Gap Analysis** — Use SEO and competitor skills to identify missing B2B topics
2. **Content Planning** — Prioritize topics by search volume, competition, and funnel stage
3. **Content Creation** — Generate blog posts following Shopplaza content rules
4. **Internal Linking** — Apply B2B internal linking strategy to connect new content
5. **Social Distribution** — Create social posts to promote new content
6. **Performance Review** — Audit published content for SEO effectiveness

## Expected Output

- `SEO-AUDIT.md` — content gap analysis
- `COMPETITOR-REPORT.md` — competitor content strategy analysis
- Blog post drafts (saved to working directory)
- `SOCIAL-CALENDAR.md` — distribution calendar for new content

Save outputs to `outputs/shopplaza/`.
